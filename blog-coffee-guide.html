8 Coffee Spots to Try in Ninh Bình

A good trip deserves a few unhurried coffee breaks. These eight places are on our homestay's suggested list — a mix of cafés and food-and-drinks stops to explore between your Ninh Bình adventures.

01

The Lab Coffee

If you enjoy a contemporary café setting, put The Lab on your list. Its modern, industrial look makes a change of pace from a day spent exploring temples and limestone landscapes. Settle in for a drink and give your itinerary a little breathing room.

02

The Lim Coffee

The Lim is another stop to consider when exploring Ninh Bình town. Visitors mention its spacious setting and range of coffee and tea drinks. Bring a friend, choose something from the menu and make time for a longer catch-up.

Open in Google Maps
03

Chookie's Beer Garden

For a change from coffee, Chookie's is a food-and-drinks stop known for craft beer. Consider it when your group wants to turn a drinks break into a meal. It's a different kind of pause in the day, especially if you're exploring with friends.

Open in Google Maps
04

Cafe Tâm Đạo Quán

Add Tâm Đạo Quán to your café shortlist for a taste of the local coffee scene. Take a seat, browse the menu and enjoy a slower moment between sightseeing stops. Open the map below to see its latest photographs and decide whether it fits your route.

05

Tam Cốc Mountain Side Coffee

A coffee break with a landscape in mind: this Tam Cốc stop is noted for its mountain setting. Consider pairing it with your time exploring the area, and leave room to sit rather than rushing on to the next sight. Check the map photos for a look at the surroundings.

Open in Google Maps
06

Peaceful Zone Coffee & Restaurant

Want to combine a drink with a food stop? Peaceful Zone offers both coffee and restaurant options, so it belongs on the shortlist when your group needs more than a quick cup. Browse the current menu before heading over and build a relaxed break into your day.

Open in Google Maps
07

Coffee Lan — Drink and Food

Coffee Lan is another option for travelers looking for drinks and food in one stop. Save it to your map and compare it with the rest of your day's route. A little flexibility helps: choose a drink, check what's available to eat and take a comfortable break.

08

RƠM VÀNG

RƠM VÀNG rounds out our homestay's suggested list. Save the pin for a coffee stop during your stay, or ask us to help fit it into your plans. The latest map photos and visitor updates are a useful starting point before you set off.
